## Q2. Interplanar spacing in FCC nickel

**Question:** Nickel has FCC structure with a lattice parameter of 3.52 Å. Find the interplanar spacing between the closest packed planes (111) in the metal.

**Options**

- (a) 1.05 Å
- (b) 1.87 Å
- (c) 2.03 Å
- (d) 3.03 Å

**Correct option:** (c) 2.03 Å

**Formulas:**

Given a=3.52\,\text{Å}, planes (111).
\[
d_{hkl}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}
\]
\[
d_{111}=\frac{3.52}{\sqrt3}\approx2.03\,\text{Å}
\]

**Explanation:**

Given a=3.52\,\text{Å}, planes (111).

For a cubic crystal:
\[
d_{hkl}=\frac{a}{\sqrt{h^2+k^2+l^2}}
\]
\[
d_{111}=\frac{3.52}{\sqrt3}\approx2.03\,\text{Å}
\]

**Answer: (c) 2.03\,\text{Å}**

## Q3. Volume change during BCC-to-FCC transition

**Question:** Iron has BCC structure below 910°C and FCC structure above this temperature. Assume the same atomic radius in both structures. Find the percentage change in volume, when the metal undergoes the structural change at 910°C.

**Options**

- (a) Volume is contract by 8.14%
- (b) Volume is increases by 10.24%
- (c) Volume is contract by 83.12%
- (d) Volume is increases by 23.14%

**Correct option:** (a) Volume contracts by 8.14%

**Formulas:**

BCC: a_{\rm BCC}=4r/\sqrt3, with 2 atoms per cell:
\[
V_{\rm BCC/atom}=\frac{a_{\rm BCC}^3}{2}
=\frac{32r^3}{3\sqrt3}
\]
FCC: a_{\rm FCC}=2\sqrt2r, with 4 atoms per cell:
\[
V_{\rm FCC/atom}=\frac{a_{\rm FCC}^3}{4}=4\sqrt2r^3
\]
\[
\%\Delta V=\frac{V_{\rm FCC/atom}-V_{\rm BCC/atom}}
{V_{\rm BCC/atom}}\times100\approx-8.14\%
\]

**Explanation:**

Assume the atomic radius r is unchanged.

BCC: a_{\rm BCC}=4r/\sqrt3, with 2 atoms per cell:
\[
V_{\rm BCC/atom}=\frac{a_{\rm BCC}^3}{2}
=\frac{32r^3}{3\sqrt3}
\]

FCC: a_{\rm FCC}=2\sqrt2r, with 4 atoms per cell:
\[
V_{\rm FCC/atom}=\frac{a_{\rm FCC}^3}{4}=4\sqrt2r^3
\]

\[
\%\Delta V=\frac{V_{\rm FCC/atom}-V_{\rm BCC/atom}}
{V_{\rm BCC/atom}}\times100\approx-8.14\%
\]
The negative sign means the volume contracts.

**Answer: (a) Volume contracts by 8.14%**

## Q4. Radius ratio for CsCl

Where r is the radius of the cation and R is the radius of the anion. CsCl structure can be viewed as a BCC lattice with atoms of one type (anion) at the corners and the atoms of the other type (cation) at the body centers.

**Options**

- (a) 0.023
- (b) 0.329
- (c) 0.545
- (d) 0.732

**Correct option:** (d) 0.732

**Formulas:**

Corner anions touch along the cube edge, so a=2R. The body-centred cation touches the corner anions along the body diagonal:
\[
r+R=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a=\sqrt3R
\]
\[
\frac rR=\sqrt3-1\approx0.732
\]

**Explanation:**

Corner anions touch along the cube edge, so a=2R. The body-centred cation touches the corner anions along the body diagonal:
\[
r+R=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}a=\sqrt3R
\]
\[
\frac rR=\sqrt3-1\approx0.732
\]

**Answer: (d) 0.732**

## Q5. Density of CsCl

**Question:** Calculate the density of CsCl given that radii of the Cs⁺ ion = 1.65 Å and for Cl⁻ ion = 1.81 Å. Atomic weights of Cs and Cl are 132.9 gm/mol and 35.45 gm/mol respectively. Consider one formula unit contain within the unit cell.

**Options**

- (a) 4.4 \times 10^3\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}
- (b) 5.5 \times 10^3\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}
- (c) 6.6 \times 10^3\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}
- (d) 7.7\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}

**Correct option:** (a) 4.4 \times 10^3\ \mathrm{kg/m^3}

**Formulas:**

Given r_{\rm Cs^+}=1.65\,\text{Å}, r_{\rm Cl^-}=1.81\,\text{Å}, and molar mass M=132.9+35.45=168.35\,\text{g/mol}. CsCl has Z=1 formula unit per unit cell.
\[
\sqrt3a=2(r_{\rm Cs^+}+r_{\rm Cl^-})
\]
\[
a=\frac{2(1.65+1.81)}{\sqrt3}\approx4.00\,\text{Å}
\]
\[
\rho=\frac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}
\approx\frac{168.35}{(6.022\times10^{23})(4.00\times10^{-8})^3}
\]
Since 1\,\text{g/cm}^3=10^3\,\text{kg/m}^3:
\[
\rho\approx4.4\times10^3\,\text{kg/m}^3
\]

**Explanation:**

Given r_{\rm Cs^+}=1.65\,\text{Å}, r_{\rm Cl^-}=1.81\,\text{Å}, and molar mass M=132.9+35.45=168.35\,\text{g/mol}. CsCl has Z=1 formula unit per unit cell.

Ions touch along the body diagonal:
\[
\sqrt3a=2(r_{\rm Cs^+}+r_{\rm Cl^-})
\]
\[
a=\frac{2(1.65+1.81)}{\sqrt3}\approx4.00\,\text{Å}
\]

\[
\rho=\frac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}
\approx\frac{168.35}{(6.022\times10^{23})(4.00\times10^{-8})^3}
\approx4.4\,\text{g/cm}^3
\]
Since 1\,\text{g/cm}^3=10^3\,\text{kg/m}^3:
\[
\rho\approx4.4\times10^3\,\text{kg/m}^3
\]

**Answer: (a) 4.4\times10^3\,\text{kg/m}^3**

## Q6. Crystal structure of cesium

**Question:** Cesium metal (atomic weight = 130) has a cubic unit cell of side 6 Å. If the density of Cs is 2 gm/cm³, determine the type of its crystal structure.

**Options**

- (a) Simple cubic structure
- (b) Face centred cubic structure
- (c) Body centred cubic structure
- (d) Hexagonal closed packed structure

**Correct option:** (c) Body centred cubic structure

**Formulas:**

Given M=130\,\text{g/mol}, a=6\,\text{Å}=6\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}, \rho=2\,\text{g/cm}^3.
\[
\rho=\frac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}
Z=\frac{\rho N_Aa^3}{M}
\]
\[
Z=\frac{2(6.022\times10^{23})(6\times10^{-8})^3}{130}
\]

**Explanation:**

Given M=130\,\text{g/mol}, a=6\,\text{Å}=6\times10^{-8}\,\text{cm}, \rho=2\,\text{g/cm}^3.

\[
\rho=\frac{ZM}{N_Aa^3}
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
Z=\frac{\rho N_Aa^3}{M}
\]
\[
Z=\frac{2(6.022\times10^{23})(6\times10^{-8})^3}{130}
\approx2
\]
Two atoms per cubic unit cell corresponds to BCC.

**Answer: (c) Body-centred cubic (BCC)**

## Q10. Conduction-electron density in FCC

**Question:** A solid with FCC-structure has a lattice constant 4 Å. Each atom in the solid has a mass of 4 × 10⁻²⁶ kg. Calculate the number of conduction electrons in 1 m³ volume of the solid, if each of the atoms of the solid contributes two electrons to its conduction band.

**Options**

- (a) 1.57 \times 10^{28}/\mathrm{m^3}
- (b) 1.25 \times 10^{29}/\mathrm{m^3}
- (c) 2.21 \times 10^{30}/\mathrm{m^3}
- (d) 4.45 \times 10^{32}/\mathrm{m^3}

**Correct option:** (b) 1.25 \times 10^{29}/\mathrm{m^3}

**Formulas:**

Given a=4\,\text{Å}=4\times10^{-10}\,\text{m}, 4 atoms per FCC unit cell, and 2 conduction electrons per atom.
\[
V_{\rm cell}=a^3=(4\times10^{-10})^3=6.4\times10^{-29}\,\text{m}^3
\]
\[
n_{\rm atoms}=\frac4{6.4\times10^{-29}}
=6.25\times10^{28}\,\text{m}^{-3}
\]
\[
n_e=2n_{\rm atoms}=1.25\times10^{29}\,\text{m}^{-3}
\]

**Explanation:**

Given a=4\,\text{Å}=4\times10^{-10}\,\text{m}, 4 atoms per FCC unit cell, and 2 conduction electrons per atom.

\[
V_{\rm cell}=a^3=(4\times10^{-10})^3=6.4\times10^{-29}\,\text{m}^3
\]
\[
n_{\rm atoms}=\frac4{6.4\times10^{-29}}
=6.25\times10^{28}\,\text{m}^{-3}
\]
\[
n_e=2n_{\rm atoms}=1.25\times10^{29}\,\text{m}^{-3}
\]

**Answer: (b) 1.25\times10^{29}\,\text{m}^{-3}**
