## Q1. Drift Velocity in a Copper Wire

A copper wire of 1 mm diameter carries a current of (5 mA). Calculate
the average drift velocity of the free electrons. Copper is monovalent
and crystallizes in FCC structure with a lattice parameter of 3.61 Å.

**Options**

- (a) (3.42 \times 10\^{-6},\text{m/s})
- (b) (4.68 \times 10\^{-7},\text{m/s})
- (c) (5.82
\times 10\^{-9},\text{m/s})
- (d) (6.17
\times 10\^{-10},\text{m/s})

**Correct option:** (b) (4.68 \times 10\^{-7},\text{m/s})

**Formulas:**

\[
v_d=\frac{I}{neA},\quad A=\frac{\pi d^2}{4},\quad n=`\frac{4}{a^3}
\]

FCC has 4 atoms per unit cell.

\[
n\approx8.50\times10^{28},\text{m}^{-3},\quad
A=7.854\times10^{-7},\text{m}^2 \]

\[
v_d=`\frac{5\times10^{-3}}{(8.50\times10^{28})(1.602\times10^{-19})(7.854\times10^{-7})}
\approx4.68\times10\^{-7},`\text{m/s} \]
