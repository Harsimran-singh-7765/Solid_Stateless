**Explanation:**

**Question type:** Drude model; relaxation time from conductivity.

**Given:** (n=8.5\times10^{28},\text{m}^{-3}),
(\sigma=6\times10^7,\Omega^{-1}\text{m}\^{-1}),
(m_e=9.1\times10\^{-31},\text{kg}),
